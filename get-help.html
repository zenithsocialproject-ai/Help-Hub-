Frequently Asked Questions

💬 Is this really free?
Yes! 100% free for anyone seeking help. You only pay standard SMS rates from your phone carrier (usually free with most plans).
🔒 Is my information private?
Absolutely. Your conversations are private and secure. We never share your personal information without your permission.
📱 Do I need a smartphone?
No! HelpHub works on any phone that can send and receive text messages, including flip phones.
⏰ How fast will I get a response?
Most responses happen within 60 seconds. Our AI system works 24/7 to match you with services instantly.
🌍 What areas do you cover?
We're currently serving [Your Service Area]. Text us your zip code to see if we cover your location!
❓ What if I don't know what I need?
No problem! Just text "HELP" and our AI will ask you questions to figure out the best services for your situation.
🆘 What if it's an emergency?
If you're in immediate danger, call 911. HelpHub is for connecting with social services, not emergency response.

🚨 In a Crisis?

HelpHub helps you find social services, but if you're in an emergency, please use these resources immediately:

911

Police, Fire, Medical Emergency

988

Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

211

Community Resources Hotline
